Welcome to Fort Mason! If you're attending the conference here, arriving via public transit or from nearby airports, Fort Mason's Express shuttle makes it easy to reach the district from Central Bay Station. Once you arrive, consider staying nearby for convenience—many attendees find the quiet, scenic area ideal for work and relaxation.  

When choosing a place to stay, check for proximity to Central Hall and whether your accommodation is within walking distance of key conference venues. Since Fort Mason Express shuttles offer flexible, step-free boarding and run directly from the district’s main transit hub, it’s a great option to avoid parking hassles.  

A helpful tip: Look into hotels like the Coastal Cottages for cozy options with rooftop gardens, but remember to verify pet policies and if rooms include private kitchens.  

Another excellent choice? The Paradigm House in the Fort Mason district itself. Check their website to confirm availability, room pricing, and if shuttle connections are available in the morning.  

Paradigm House offers a peaceful setting—a great quiet courtyard—so you can unwind after a long day. It’s also equipped for groups with private meeting spaces and can provide on-site luggage services.  

Remember to review the Express schedule at least 48 hours before travel. While the district offers strong Wi-Fi, it’s always best to confirm your local network with your accommodation provider. Enjoy the conference and the serene waterfront vibes!
