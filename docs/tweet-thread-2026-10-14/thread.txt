4/9 A small continuation rule helps a winning bidder finish a phrase: up to 3 extra tokens, free, while its raw next-token confidence stays at least 98%. These carried tokens bypass watermark sampling, but the decoder still scores them. That can weaken recovery.

Caption note: The arrows mark free continuation tokens. This run contains 52 carried tokens.
Alt text (03-tokens.png): Token ribbon for the same response. Blue and orange indicate bidder branches, white indicates shared tokens, and arrows indicate free carried tokens. The run includes 52 carried tokens.

6/9 The useful change: mark each brand's first eligible mention once, then repeat evidence for that 16-word region across later chunks of text. Spare capacity reinforces existing records instead of encoding every mention again. Two regions can overlap.

Attach: 05b-watermark-evidence.png
Caption note: Actual decoder evidence: 242 scored tokens support the shuttle region; 71 support the hotel region. These are evidence modes and can share scored positions.
Alt text (05b-watermark-evidence.png): Expanded evidence for the two detected regions. Shuttle region: 242 scored later tokens across both lanes. Hotel region: 71 scored later tokens in lane 1. Nominal p-values are explicitly not calibrated confidence percentages.
